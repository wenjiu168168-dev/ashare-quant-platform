# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

Contributors should communicate respectfully, focus on evidence, and prioritize safety, reproducibility, and risk transparency.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, abuse, or discriminatory language.
- Misrepresenting backtest or test results.
- Claiming guaranteed investment returns.
- Publishing secrets or credentials.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may reject contributions that violate project safety, security, or research-integrity rules.
